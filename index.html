Privacy Policy — BF Wiki

Last updated: 28 August 2026

BF Wiki ("the app") is a fan-made reference app. This policy explains what data the app and the third-party services it relies on collect, and why.

No account, no personal profile

The app has no sign-in, no user accounts, and does not ask for your name, email, or any personal profile information. The wiki content itself (units, skills, items) is stored in a local database on your device and is not tied to you in any way.

Analytics and crash reporting

The app uses Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics (Google) to understand how the app is used and to diagnose crashes. This can include device information, app version, general usage events (like which screens are opened), and crash logs. See Firebase's privacy policy.

Advertising

Unless you've supported the app (see below), the app shows ads served by Google AdMob. Depending on the consent choice you make when prompted, ads may be personalized (using an advertising identifier) or non-personalized. On iOS, personalized ads additionally require your permission under Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework — you can change this at any time in your device's Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. You can also revisit your ad consent choice from within the app's Settings screen. See Google's advertising privacy policy.

Purchases

If you choose to support the app (which removes ads), purchases are processed by Apple's App Store or Google Play, and purchase state is managed through RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous identifier and your purchase receipt to verify entitlement — no payment details (card numbers, etc.) ever pass through the app or RevenueCat. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Data sharing

Data described above is shared only with the service providers named in this policy (Google/Firebase/AdMob, RevenueCat, and Apple/Google as the app store operator) to provide analytics, crash reporting, advertising, and purchase functionality. It is never sold.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will be revised.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent via the "Send feedback" option in the app's Settings screen.
